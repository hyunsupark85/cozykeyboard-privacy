# CozyKeyboard Guide

CozyKeyboard is a Korean/English keyboard for iPhone that translates what you type, right above the keys.

## Set up (about one minute)

1. **Turn on the keyboard.** Open the CozyKeyboard app and tap **Open Settings**, then **Keyboards**. Turn on **CozyKeyboard** and **Allow Full Access**, and tap **Allow** when iOS asks.
2. **Connect an engine (optional).** Without any setup, CozyKeyboard translates with Apple AI on your iPhone. If you have an API key for an AI service (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and others), tap **Connect** next to it and paste the key. An API key is a personal code you create on that company's developer page. It is separate from an app subscription and is billed by how much you use.
3. **Remove other keyboards (optional).** In the Settings app go to **General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Edit** and delete the others. CozyKeyboard then opens every time. Password fields still use Apple's keyboard.
4. **Try it.** If Apple's keyboard shows up, hold its 🌐 and choose CozyKeyboard.

## Use

- **Translate:** type and pause. The translation appears above the keys. Tap it (or ⇄) to replace your text.
- **Undo:** tap ↩︎ to bring your original text back.
- **Languages:** tap the language chips to choose languages, and ↔ to swap the direction.
- **Tone:** tap the tone chip for Business, Neutral, or Casual.
- **Read a message:** copy it, then tap the clipboard button to read it in your language.
- **Engine and model:** tap the engine chip to switch between your connected engines and their models.
- **Korean / English keys:** tap 🌐. Hold 🌐 to hide or show the translation bar.
- **Emoji:** tap 😀. If you have other keyboards turned on, hold 😀 to switch to the next one.
- **Close the keyboard:** swipe down on the translation bar.
- **Move the cursor:** hold space and drag.

## Languages

- **Keys you type with:** Korean (Hangul) and English.
- **Translation, in either direction between any two of:** Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified), Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil).

For example, type in Korean and send in Japanese, or copy a French message and read it in Korean.

## Good to know

- Automatic translation is off in fields for email addresses, numbers, codes, and logins.
- If an engine runs out of quota or is overloaded, CozyKeyboard uses your next connected engine, then Apple AI. The engine chip shows which one translated.
- Without Full Access the keyboard still types, and nothing you type leaves your iPhone.
- Requires iOS 27 or later.
