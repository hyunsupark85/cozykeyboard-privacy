# CozyKeyboard Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

## The short version

CozyKeyboard has no server, no account, no ads, and no analytics. The developer never receives anything you type.

## What leaves your iPhone

When you translate with an AI engine you connected (for example Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini), the text being translated is sent directly from your iPhone to that company, using the API key you entered. That company handles it under its own privacy policy and the terms of your account with it. CozyKeyboard asks for your permission before you connect each engine.

Nothing is sent when you use Apple AI or offline translation. Those run on your iPhone.

Text is sent only for translation: when you pause typing in an ordinary text field, when you tap the translate button, or when you tap the clipboard button to translate copied text. Automatic translation is off in fields for email addresses, web addresses, numbers, codes, logins, and other autofill details. iOS always uses Apple's keyboard for password fields.

## What stays on your iPhone

Your API keys are kept in the iOS Keychain on this device. Your settings and your recently used emoji are kept in the app's storage. What you type is never written to storage or to logs. Translations are remembered only in memory while the keyboard is open.

## Full Access

iOS requires "Allow Full Access" before a keyboard can use the network. CozyKeyboard uses it only to reach the engine you chose and to read your keys. Without Full Access the keyboard still types, and nothing you type leaves your iPhone.

## Your choices

Remove Key in Settings disconnects an engine and deletes its key. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. To ask an AI company to delete data it holds, use that company's own tools.

## Children

CozyKeyboard is not directed at children under 13.
